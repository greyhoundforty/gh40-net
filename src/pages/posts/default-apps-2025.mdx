Inspired by a recent listen to the [Hemispheric Views](https://listen.hemisphericviews.com/097) podcast I set about creating a list of my default apps as I start 2025. I wanted to catalog these and then revisit them at the end of the year to see how my usage has changed. I am doing something similar with my [homelab setup](https://www.ryantiffany.com/2024-homelab-recap/) as I way to get more insight into what my day to day working environment looks like.  

If you are looking for more `Default Apps` lists, [Rob Knight](https://rknight.me) has put together a page great curated list [here](https://defaults.rknight.me)

## Default Apps

- 📨 **Mail Client**: Spark 
- 📮 **Mail Server**: Fastmail
- 📝 **Notes**: Drafts
- ✅ **To-Do**: Doing
- 📷 **Photo Shooting**: iPhone 15
- 🎨 **Photo Management**: Photos
- 📆 **Calendar**: Fantastical
- 📁 **Cloud File Storage**: iCloud
- 📖 **RSS**: NetNewsWire
- 🚀 **Launcher**: Raycast
- 🙍🏻‍♂️ **Contacts**: Apple Contacts
- 🌐 **Browser**: Zen (Safari on iOS)
- 💬 **Chat**: iMessage, Discord
- 🔖 **Bookmarks**: Linkwarden
- 📑 **Read It Later**: Safari Reading List (probably need something more robust)
- 📜 **Word Processing**: Drafts
- 📈 **Spreadsheets**: Excel (mainly because of work)
- 📊 **Presentations**: Powerpoint (ditto for work)
- 🛒 **Shopping Lists**: Apple Notes (shared with family)
- 🍴 **Meal Planning**: None 🚫
- 💰 **Budgeting and Personal Finance**:  MoneyWiz
- 📰 **News**: None 🚫 (at this point in time I'd rather stick my head in a beehive and yell)
- 🎵 **Music**: Apple Music (jumped mostly off of Spotify late 2024 due to lack of good discovery)
- 🎤 **Podcasts**: Overcast
- 🔐 **Password Management**: Apple Passwords (moving away from 1password slowly)
- 🌤️ **Weather**: Apple Weather
- 🔎 **Search**: DuckDuckGo
- 🧭 Maps: Apple Maps
- 🧑‍💻 **Code Editor**: VSCode
- ✈️ **VPN**: Tailscale
